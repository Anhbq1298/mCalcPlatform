    Calculation basis

    The elastic bolt group distributes the direct forces equally and distributes moment in proportion to each bolt’s distance from the centroid. Plan skew leaves vertical shear unchanged. Normal eccentricity is projected onto the beam axis.

    Bolt shear is a fastener check. Beam-web and plate bearing use separate thicknesses, strengths and boundaries. The per-bolt bearing interaction is a screening calculation. Eligible standard layouts also show the SCI P358 component results separately.

    Coordinates start at the support face in elevation. Positive x points away from the support, positive y points up. The geometry mode uses the bolt centroid measured along the beam axis. The idealized plan shows the reference lines and skew.
