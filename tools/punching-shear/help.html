5 Not covered

  • Drops at edge or corner columns, drops combined with openings, and drops offset from the column.
  • A drop that is under Fig. 6.17 in one direction and Fig. 6.18 in the other (l_H straddles 2h_H).
  • Links in the slab outside the drop.
